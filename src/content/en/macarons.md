slug: macarons
title: Macarons
description: My French meringue macaron recipe, and the one step you must not miss.
category: Dessert
cuisine: French
keywords: [macarons]
ingredients:
  - 2 egg whites (~55g)
  - 66g caster sugar
  - 59g icing sugar
  - 59g almond flour
steps:
  - name: Make a meringue base
    text: Whisk the egg whites until foamy
  - name: Add half of the caster sugar
    text: Add 33g of caster sugar to the meringue
  - name: Whip the whites
    text: Keep whisking until the whites form a peak
  - name: Finish the meringue base
    text: Add the rest of the caster sugar and the food colouring. Keep whisking until the surface is smooth and even
  - name: Fold in the icing sugar and almond flour
    text: Fold in the icing sugar and almond flour mixture *slowly* with a spatula, always in the same direction
  - name: Pipe the shells
    text: With a piping bag, pipe the shells onto a lightly warmed baking sheet
  - name: Bake
    text: Bake for 12 minutes at 300°F
  - name: Assemble the shells
    text: Let cool, lift the shells off the sheet, and sandwich them with the ganache of your choice
  - name: Enjoy
    text: Bon appétit!
---

Macarons always make an impression. The first batches will probably fail, at least in part. What really matters is getting the right texture when you add the almond flour.
