---
slug: canneles
title: Cannelés
description: My recipe for Bordeaux cannelés, with rum and vanilla.
category: Dessert
cuisine: French
keywords: [cannelés, canelés]
ingredients:
  - 240g sugar
  - 125g flour
  - 50cl milk
  - 50g butter
  - 3 yolks and 2 whole eggs
  - 2 tablespoons of rum
  - 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract
steps:
  - name: Make the batter
    text: Mix the ingredients in the order listed above
  - name: Fill the cannelé mould
    text: Fill each cavity of the mould halfway
  - name: Bake — No convection oven!
    text: Start baking at 425°F/220°C for 10 minutes
  - name: Lower the temperature
    text: Lower the temperature to 355°F/180°C for 50 minutes
  - name: Rest in the warm oven
    text: Turn the oven off and let the cannelés rest for 10 minutes
  - name: Enjoy
    text: Bon appétit!
---

Cannelés are a specialty of Bordeaux. The batter is easy to make, but the baking is somewhat tricky. Stick to the times and temperatures, or the cannelés will come out underbaked.
