---
slug: crepes
title: Crêpe Batter
description: My best crêpe batter, for thin and tender crêpes.
category: Pastry
cuisine: Brunch
keywords: [crêpes, brunch]
ingredients:
  - 250g cake flour
  - 4 large eggs
  - 500ml milk
  - 1 pinch of salt
  - 10g icing sugar
  - 2 tablespoons of orange blossom extract
  - 1 tablespoon of dark rum (optional)
steps:
  - name: Sift the flour into a mixing bowl
    text: An essential step to avoid lumps!
  - name: Combine the dry ingredients
    text: Start beating while adding the salt and sugar
  - name: Add the eggs one at a time
    text: While still beating, add the eggs one at a time, until you get a sticky batter
  - name: Mix the liquids
    text: Mix the milk with the orange blossom extract and the rum
  - name: Add the flavoured milk
    text: Pour the milk very slowly onto the batter while still beating. Gradually raise the mixer speed. Keep beating until the batter is smooth
  - name: Let the batter rest
    text: At least 2 hours, ideally overnight in the fridge
  - name: Cook
    text: Cook in a lightly buttered crêpe pan over medium heat. Flip halfway through
  - name: Enjoy
    text: Bon appétit!
---
