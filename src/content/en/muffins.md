---
slug: english-muffins
title: English Muffins
description: Muffins toasted in a frying pan.
category: Bakery
cuisine: Brunch
keywords: [english muffins, brunch]
ingredients:
  - group: Bowl 1
    items:
      - 30cl milk
      - 10g yeast
      - 1 teaspoon of sugar
  - group: Bowl 2
    items:
      - 600g flour
      - 30g soft butter
      - 1 pinch of salt
  - 10cl water
steps:
  - name: Combine the ingredients
    text: In one bowl, mix the milk, the yeast, and the sugar. In another bowl, the flour, the butter, and the salt
  - name: Knead the dough
    text: Combine the contents of both bowls with 10cl of water
  - name: Let the dough rise
    text: 2h at room temperature, or ideally overnight in the fridge
  - name: Shape the muffins
    text: Cut the dough into pieces, roll them into balls, and let them rise for another 2 hours
  - name: Cook
    text: Cook in a covered frying pan over medium heat for 5 minutes. Flip halfway through
  - name: Enjoy
    text: Bon appétit!
---

These English muffins are sublime. Keep some fine semolina on hand to rest them in if you want a little more texture.
