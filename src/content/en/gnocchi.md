---
slug: gnocchi
title: Gnocchi
description: My basic gnocchi recipe.
category: Pasta
cuisine: Italian
keywords: [gnocchi]
ingredients:
  - 1 pound of potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet)
  - 1 1/2 cups of flour (Tipo 00)
  - 1/2 teaspoon of salt
steps:
  - name: Boil the potatoes
    text: In a large pot of water, put the potatoes in the cold water and bring it gently to a boil
  - name: Cool the potatoes
    text: Once cooked, let the potatoes cool in a bowl. Cut them into pieces to speed up the cooling
  - name: Mash
    text: Using a food mill, mash the potatoes into a smooth purée
  - name: Mix with the flour and salt
    text: Knead the purée with the flour until the dough is smooth
  - name: Rest for 20 min
  - name: Shape the gnocchi
    text: Roll the dough into ropes. Cut them into pieces. Roll the pieces over a gnocchi board, or a fork if you have none
  - name: Cook
    text: 2 min in salted boiling water, until the gnocchi float to the surface
  - name: Enjoy
    text: Bon appétit!
---

After several attempts, this recipe is the best. Mashing with a food mill is essential to get the right texture. Fresh gnocchi do not keep long.
