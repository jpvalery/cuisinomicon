slug: fresh-pasta
title: Fresh Pasta
description: My basic fresh pasta recipe.
category: Pasta
cuisine: Italian
keywords: [fresh pasta]
ingredients:
  - 2 cups of flour (Tipo 00)
  - 3 large eggs
  - 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon of olive oil
steps:
  - name: Knead the dough
    text: On a work surface, make a well in the flour. Add the eggs. Gradually beat them in with a fork until the dough is smooth
  - name: Let the dough rest
    text: Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and let it rest for 30 minutes
  - name: Use a pasta machine to roll out and cut the dough
  - name: Cook
    text: 2 min in salted boiling water
  - name: Enjoy
    text: Bon appétit!
---

Fresh pasta has an exceptional texture and taste.
